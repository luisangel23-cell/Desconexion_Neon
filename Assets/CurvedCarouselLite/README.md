# Curved Carousel Menu Lite — FREE

**CodeStart-AI · v1.0.0 · Unity 6 · uGUI · TextMeshPro · Input System**

A free curved (arc) carousel menu for Unity UI: drag, swipe, click, Previous/Next and arrow keys, with smooth snapping, looping and a highlighted center card.

**Try it:** open `Demo/Scenes/CurvedCarouselLiteDemo.unity` and press **Play**.

**Use it:** right-click a Canvas ▸ **UI ▸ CodeStart-AI ▸ Curved Carousel Lite**, add your images in *Content ▸ Items*, press **Play**.
